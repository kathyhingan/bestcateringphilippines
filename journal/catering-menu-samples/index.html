Start from a sample,
not a blank page.

Start here

A blank menu is where event planning stalls, and a finished one is where it speeds up. Catering menu samples hand you a working line-up that has already been served, ordered course by course, so instead of inventing dishes you are judging them: does this table carry enough viands for 100 guests, is there a soup course at a sit-down wedding, does the dessert survive a garden setup. The catering menu samples Philippines caterers hand out at bridal fairs and post on their pages are exactly this, a served menu wearing its best clothes.

A sample also teaches you the shape of a Filipino spread faster than any definition. Rice and noodles anchor the table, two to four viands share the spotlight by protein, vegetables and salad sit beside them, dessert and drinks close the line. Once you can read that shape, every quotation you receive becomes a set of substitutions on a menu you already understand, which is what keeps comparisons honest.

This guide walks the sample line-ups by occasion, separates the dishes from the package that carries them, explains why a sample with printed prices is a starting figure rather than a quote, and hands you a free working template to plan your own line-up before you inquire.

Sample line-ups
by occasion.

By occasion

The occasion decides the line-up before the budget does. The patterns below are the ones a sample menu list for catering in the Philippines almost always follows:

Weddings. A carving station or roast as the centerpiece, soup course, three to four viands, pasta or pancit, a dressed vegetable, dessert bar or plated dessert, and bottomless drinks. The fullest line-up on any caterer's sheet, because the wedding table is the one guests remember.

Debuts. Close to the wedding menu with a cocktail hour in front of it: canapes and a drinks station while the program runs, then a shorter plated or buffet course list behind it.

Christenings and birthdays. A friendlier per-head line-up: one or two viands, pancit for long life, a themed cake or dessert station, and juice or iced tea by the pitcher. Finger foods carry the children's table.

Corporate events. Packed meals or a compact buffet that fits a midday window, with coffee service and a clean dessert that survives a conference room.

Christmas and New Year. The handaan table: lechon or ham as the centerpiece, the braised pots, fruit salad and queso de bola, and the drinks that keep the program going past midnight.

Full course lists for all eight occasions, with the portions behind each line, sit in the catering menu guide. When you read any sample against your own event, check the guest count the line-up was planned for before you check the dishes, because scaling a 50-guest spread to 300 changes the cooking, the staffing and the quote.

Packages, per-head menus
and what the bundle includes.

Read the bundle

The dishes are only half of any quotation. Catering menu packages wrap the menu inside a service bundle: the buffet tables and setup, guest tables and chairs with centerpieces, the service staff, and sometimes freebies like a cake or a tarpaulin, all inside one per-head rate. A menu sample lists the food alone. Comparing a package against a bare menu is the most common mistake in the inquiry stage, and it is why two caterers at the same per-head figure can leave two very different rooms behind them.

The catering services menu package behind a rate usually names its inclusions on the quote: how many chafing dishes, what linen grade, how many attendants per 50 guests, and which menu lines sit inside the rate. The sample menu catering packages advertise on social media is the food half of that bundle; always ask for the other half in writing before you compare figures.

Filipino catering menu packages add the occasion logic on top: wedding bundles price the fullest course list, debut bundles add the cocktail hour, and kiddie party bundles run short line-ups with the service trimmed to match. The pricing explainer breaks down every factor that moves the per-head rate, so the package you compare is the package you actually get.

What a sample catering menu
with prices can and cannot tell you.

No published prices

Samples circulate with printed figures, and a sample catering menu with prices attached is useful for one thing only: seeing what drives the number. The per-head rate moves with the guest count, the venue, the service style and the date, and a printed price cannot see any of the four. A 150-peso-per-head line-up that reads affordable for a 30-guest christening is a different dish entirely at 300 guests in a tagaytay garden with a long program.

Any sample catering menu with prices Philippines websites publish is a teaching figure, not a quote, and caterers who refuse to print prices are usually protecting the accuracy of the number they will eventually put in writing. What you can take from a priced sample is the structure: which lines price per head, which items like lechon or a second station price separately, and where the add-ons hide.

That is the reading this site teaches, and it is also why every quote here is built from your occasion, guest count, venue and date rather than a menu with a number stapled to it. Send those four facts and the quote you get back prices the exact line-up for the exact day.

Build your own working
menu template.

Free template

Before you inquire, build the sheet a caterer will thank you for. Any menu for catering template needs four parts: a heading with the occasion, guest count, venue and date; a table of course lines with the dish, the count or portion basis and notes; a separate section for by-weight or by-tray items; and a service notes block for setup, staffing and timeline. A food catering menu template is not a design exercise, it is a working document, and a clean one-page table is all it takes.

Free catering menu templates are everywhere online, but the version that helps most is the one you build yourself, because building it is how you notice the gaps: no soup course for a sit-down wedding, one dessert for 200 guests, no drinks plan for an outdoor program. If you want to work in a document processor, a catering menu template Word file is just a table with those columns; save a master copy and duplicate it per event so every version keeps its own history.

Bring the finished sheet to your inquiry. A caterer who receives an occasion, a guest count and a line-up with basis notes can price a real menu in one pass instead of two, and the quote that comes back will read like your own plan priced honestly rather than a stranger's guess.

Common questions.

Menu sample FAQs

What is included in a catering menu sample?
A catering menu sample lays out the dishes a caterer serves in a working order: rice and noodle stations, the viands by protein, the vegetable and salad sides, soup where the occasion calls for one, dessert, and the drinks line. A complete sample also states the service style the line-up assumes, buffet, family style or plated, and the guest count it was planned for, because a line-up that reads well at 50 guests does not always stretch to 300.

What is the difference between a catering package menu and a per-head menu sample?
A catering package menu bundles the food with the service around it: guest tables and chairs, buffet setup, staff and sometimes venue extras, all inside one per-head rate. A menu sample on its own lists only the dishes. When you compare packages, ask which menu lines sit inside the rate and which items, like a lechon roast or a second dessert station, price as add-ons, because two packages at the same per-head figure can carry very different tables.

Where can I find Filipino catering menu packages Philippines couples actually book?
Catering menu packages Philippines caterers publish follow the occasion first: wedding packages center on a carving station and a fuller course list, debut packages add the cocktail hour, and christening or birthday packages run a shorter line-up at a friendlier per-head figure. The fastest way to a real package is to send your occasion, guest count, venue and date to a caterer and ask for the package menu they would build for that day.

Do catering menu samples come with prices?
Some do, but treat any sample catering menu with prices attached as a starting figure, not a quote. Published prices cannot see your guest count, venue, service style or date, and caterers adjust the line-up and the rate against all four. Use the sample to understand what drives the number, then request a written quote for your actual event so the price you compare is one you can hold.

Is there a free catering menu template I can use for planning?
Yes, and it is a useful planning tool even before you talk to a caterer. A free catering menu template gives you the empty structure, courses, counts and notes per line, so you can sketch what you want the table to carry and spot the gaps early. Bring that working sheet to the inquiry and the caterer can price a real menu around it faster.

How do I build a free catering menu template Word can open?
A catering menu template in Word needs very little: one page per menu version, a heading with the occasion and guest count, then a table with columns for the course, the dish, the count or portion basis and notes. Save a master copy and duplicate it per event so each version keeps its own history. Caterers work from far simpler sheets than clients expect, so a clean one-page table is already a professional working document.

What should a sample menu for catering services list per guest?
A sample menu for catering services should list every course line with its basis: rice and noodles per head, viands per head with the protein named, soup per head where served, dessert per head, drinks either bottomless or by pitcher, and any by-weight or by-tray items priced separately. Listing the basis next to each line is what makes a sample menu list for catering readable on a quote later.
