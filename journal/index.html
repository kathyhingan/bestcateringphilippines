Why the Journal is different

Most event-planning content online answers "how do I plan a wedding" in the abstract. That is not useful when you already know you are planning a debut in Cebu, or a Christmas party in Clark for a hundred people. The Journal is built the other way around: every guide crosses a specific city with a specific occasion, so what you read matches what you are actually about to do.

Ten locations and eight occasions make eighty possible combinations. We are publishing the ones with the most real demand first, three worked examples live now, more added as each Location × Occasion pairing is written.
