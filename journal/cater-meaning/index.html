How do you say it:
catering pronunciation.

Say it right

catering pronunciation is KAY-ter-ing, stressed on the first syllable. Cater is KAY-ter, with the same stress, and the vowel is the long A of "day". The word is often said as if it were "cah-ter-ing", which drifts toward the word for a water carrier, so the safest habit is to land hard on KAY.

Say the noun and the verb the same way: catering, caterer, catered, all with the KAY first. Once the stress is fixed, the rest of the trade vocabulary, pax, quote, buffet, handaan, is easy to say in the same breath.

Where the trade came from:
the history of catering.

Short history

The history of catering starts with the European banquet, where food was carried out to a table the guests never left, and it grew through hotels, railway dining cars and airlines, each of which needed food made in one place and served in another. The history of banquet and catering services widened the same story into public celebrations, and the history of catering services as a modern industry followed the hotels.

The history of catering in the philippines runs through the Spanish-era fiesta and the handaan tradition, where the whole barangay shares one long table, then the hotel and restaurant trade of the twentieth century. The Philippine version kept the family table at the centre while adding the logistics, the delivery, the equipment and the staff count, which is what a modern caterer actually sells.
