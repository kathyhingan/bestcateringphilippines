Before you ask

How does Best Catering Philippines work?

Tell us your occasion, date, location and guest count. We run your request against our private bench of vetted local caterers, get competitive bids, and quote you one clear price within 24 hours. The fulfilling caterer is managed by us from quote to event day.

How much does catering cost in the Philippines?

Philippine catering generally runs from about 700 to 1,000 pesos per head for a basic buffet, 1,000 to 1,800 pesos for a mid-range menu, and 1,800 pesos and up for premium plated service. We do not publish fixed prices; every quote is custom to your occasion, guest count, menu and location.

What is the minimum booking size?

We manage celebrations at 50 to 100+ guests with budgets from 150,000 pesos. This is the scale our vetting, bidding and guarantee are built for. Smaller celebrations are still welcomed and referred to vendors directly.

Which locations do you serve?

Ten launch locations nationwide: Metro Manila, Calabarzon, Palawan, Cebu, Baguio, La Union, Ilocos, Clark, Subic and Boracay, all live at launch with the same vetting and the same guarantee.
