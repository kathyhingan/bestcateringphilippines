Why a guarantee,
not just a promise.

Stated plainly

Best Catering Philippines has zero completed events under its own name at launch. Rather than inventing a track record we do not have, we are stating the exact process and the exact guarantee you get instead. This is what a vetted-caterer network owes you when you cannot yet lean on years of reviews.

Every other catering site you will find either shows you a portfolio of real past events, or shows you nothing at all and asks you to trust a name. We are doing something different: telling you precisely what happens if your caterer fails, precisely who shows up on your day, and precisely when your vendor gets checked before your event. Nothing here is aspirational language. It is a checklist you can hold us to.

The three commitments.

Qualified bookings, 150K+

What happens if my caterer fails close to my event date?

If your assigned caterer fails or underdelivers close to your date, we have a backup ready from our vetted bench. This is the entire point of running a bench of vetted caterers instead of a single relationship: when one falls through, there is already a second option that has been through the same vetting, for your city and your occasion. You are never left sourcing a replacement yourself with days to go.

Will someone from Best Catering Philippines be at my event?

Someone from Best Catering Philippines is present or on call during your event itself, not just handling the booking beforehand. Most booking platforms disappear the moment a contract is signed. We do not. If a chafing dish runs cold or a delivery is late, there is one number to call, and it belongs to someone who already knows your event.

How do you confirm my caterer is ready before my event?

We personally verify your caterer is prepared: headcount, menu, staff, before your event. This is a real check against your actual booking: the guest count you confirmed, the menu you selected, the staffing level the event needs, not a generic reminder email. The exact number of days before your event is being finalized; the check itself is not optional.

Does the guarantee apply to every booking?

The guarantee applies to qualified bookings from 150,000 pesos, at 50 to 100 or more guests. This is the scale the guarantee, the vetting process, and the coordination fee are built and priced for. Smaller bookings are still welcomed and referred to vendors directly, at a lighter level of involvement and a different fee structure.

The vetting and
bidding process.

How it actually works
  1. You tell us the occasion, date, location and guest count. Through the quote form or the Plan My Celebration quiz, whichever is faster for you.
  2. We run your request against our private vendor bench for that city and occasion. Real local catering companies bid competitively; we do not show you a public list because the vendor relationship is ours to manage, not yours to negotiate.
  3. We pick the winning bid and quote you one number. The vendor's price plus a flat coordination fee, no separate line items, no surprise markups later.
  4. You get one point of contact from quote to event day. Not the vendor's number, ours, so accountability never gets passed around.

Why this matters
at real scale.

50 to 100+ guests

A catering failure at a dinner party for eight is awkward. A catering failure at a wedding reception for 150, in front of every relative who flew in for the day, is a different category of risk entirely. The research on Filipino wedding catering is consistent on one point: couples spend 40 to 50 percent of their entire wedding budget on food, and guests remember the food longer than almost anything else about the day.

That is exactly the scale where an unverified caterer is a genuine gamble, not just an inconvenience. Our three commitments exist specifically for that scale: a backup vendor, a person physically accountable on the day, and a pre-event check against your actual numbers. None of this is needed for a small family lunch. All of it is needed the moment 100 people, a rented venue, and a once-in-a-lifetime occasion are on the line together.
