Planning a Debut
in Cebu.

Venues, guest counts, and what to actually expect when you are staging a full 18th debut celebration on the island.

An 18th debut in Cebu follows the same core structure as anywhere else in the Philippines: 18 roses, 18 candles, 18 treasures, a program that can run four hours or longer. What changes by location is the venue landscape, typical guest counts, and a few Cebu-specific logistics worth knowing before you book anything.

This guide is for the planning stage, before you are ready to request a quote. If you already know your date and guest count, the debut catering page and the Cebu location page cover what happens once you are ready to move forward.

Venue options in Cebu for a debut

Cebu's debut venues split between hotel ballrooms and function halls in Cebu City, and resort-style venues toward Mactan and Lapu-Lapu for families wanting a more scenic, destination-adjacent feel without leaving the island. Heritage-adjacent venues also appear in Cebu City for families wanting a more distinctive backdrop than a standard ballroom.

The choice usually comes down to guest count and formality: a hotel ballroom suits a more traditional, larger cotillion program, while a resort venue suits a family wanting photos and ambiance to carry more of the event's character.

Typical guest counts for a Cebu debut

Cebu debuts commonly run in the same range as the rest of the country, 50 to 150 guests depending on family size and how extensive the 18 roses and 18 candles list is. Larger, more traditional cotillion-style debuts tend toward the upper end of that range; more intimate family-first celebrations toward the lower end.

What to expect from catering during a long program

A cotillion-length program is the main catering consideration specific to this occasion, regardless of city. Food quality and timing need to hold across several hours, not just a single seated dinner service, which is why we specifically vet debut caterers for long-program experience rather than assuming any wedding-capable caterer can handle the format equally well.

Cebu-specific logistics worth asking about: resort venues toward Mactan may have different kitchen access and delivery timing than a Cebu City hotel, so confirm this with your venue and caterer together before finalizing your program schedule.

What things actually cost
Mactan Resorts

Resort Receptions

Mactan's resort properties (Shangri-La, Crimson, Movenpick, Jpark, Solea) host debut receptions at price points from roughly 2,500 pesos per guest for a garden cocktail setup up to 6,500 pesos and above for plated full service, with dedicated coordinators usually included in the package.

These resorts bundle venue, catering and coordination, which simplifies planning but limits menu flexibility compared to an independent caterer.

Catering watch-out: Ask whether the resort package price is per-guest with a minimum spend, and whether outside caterers are permitted at all; many Mactan properties restrict them.
Cebu City Hotels

Hotel Ballrooms

Cebu City's hotel ballrooms (Radisson Blu, Bai, Marco Polo Plaza, Waterfront) handle debut-scale events with 200 to 400 guest capacity and, unlike many Mactan resorts, usually accept outside caterers with a corkage fee.

This makes a city hotel ballroom the natural fit if you want a specific independent caterer rather than the hotel's own kitchen.

Catering watch-out: Confirm the corkage fee and what it covers (kitchen access, equipment brought in, staff meals) before comparing quotes, since it changes the real total.
Timing note

January debuts compete with Sinulog.

Cebu's Sinulog festival (mid-January) floods Cebu City with visitors and books out hotels, venues and caterers around the Basilica and the Sinulog route specifically. A debut planned for January or early February competes directly with that demand.

What this means: book 12 or more months ahead for a January-February date, or pick a venue outside the Sinulog route where January demand is closer to normal.
