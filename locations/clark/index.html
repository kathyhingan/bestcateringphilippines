What makes Clark different

Clark carries the third-highest regional family income in the country, and its venue infrastructure reflects a specific mix: resort properties, casino event spaces and MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences, exhibitions) facilities built for large-scale corporate functions, sitting alongside genuine family milestone demand from a well-off local population.

This means Clark caterers need range that a purely family-event market does not require: the professional presentation and schedule discipline a corporate conference or company Christmas party expects, and the warmth and Filipino-menu depth a wedding or debut needs, sometimes in the very same venue on different dates.

We vet Clark caterers across both registers, so whether your booking is a company event or a family celebration, the vendor matched to you actually fits the occasion.

Venue note Clark's venues range from resort and casino event spaces to dedicated MICE facilities, plus more traditional function halls for family celebrations. Tell us your venue and occasion type so we match correctly.

What actually matters here
The Freeport zone has its own gate and speed rules

NLEX-SCTEX makes Clark predictable, but the Freeport gate is its own checkpoint.

Clark's biggest logistics advantage over Metro Manila is predictability: the NLEX-SCTEX expressway connection makes the roughly 80-kilometer drive from Manila a consistent 1.5 to 2 hours in normal traffic, none of EDSA's rush-hour unpredictability. This makes on-time delivery genuinely more reliable here than in the metro itself.

Venues inside the Clark Freeport Zone specifically involve passing through a gate checkpoint, with the zone enforcing its own internal traffic rules including a lower speed limit. This is a minor addition to travel time but a real one, a caterer unfamiliar with the zone can lose extra minutes navigating an unfamiliar checkpoint process on the day itself.

What this means for your booking: confirm whether your specific venue sits inside the Freeport Zone gates or in the wider Angeles City and San Fernando area outside them, since the checkpoint only applies to the former.

Does Clark work for both corporate events and family celebrations?

Yes, Clark's venue mix genuinely supports both, and we vet local caterers for each register separately: corporate schedule discipline for MICE and company events, warmth and traditional menus for family milestones.

How much does catering cost in Clark?

General Philippine market rates apply, roughly 700 to 1,800 pesos per head for family events, and around 345 to 1,250 pesos per person for standard corporate catering formats. We do not publish fixed prices; every quote is custom.

Can you cater a large MICE or conference event in Clark?

Yes, our bench includes caterers experienced with Clark's MICE and conference venue logistics specifically, alongside resort and casino event spaces.

Do you also serve Angeles City and San Fernando?

Yes, our Clark coverage extends to Angeles City, San Fernando, Mabalacat, Apalit and the surrounding Pampanga area. Tell us your specific venue location when you request a quote.
