What makes Metro Manila different

Metro Manila has the largest concentration of catering companies anywhere in the Philippines, spread across Quezon City, Makati, Marikina, Paranaque and every district in between. That is genuinely useful: more real capacity, more menu styles, more venues built specifically to host large events.

It also means the widest spread in quality and reliability. A search for a Metro Manila caterer turns up dozens of options with no easy way to tell which ones can actually deliver at 100-plus guests versus which ones are better suited to a small office lunch. The volume of choice is exactly why vetting matters most here, not least.

We draw from a private bench of caterers already checked for capacity across Metro Manila's major districts, so the volume of options works in your favor instead of against you.

Venue note Metro Manila's event venues range from hotel ballrooms in Makati and BGC to garden and estate venues on the city's edges. Your vetted caterer is matched to your specific venue type, not a one-size setup.

What actually matters here
The traffic reality

EDSA doesn't care about your event timeline.

Metro Manila's defining logistics problem is not distance, it is unpredictability. A 20-minute drive can become a 90-minute crawl once EDSA, C5, or Ortigas Avenue hit rush hour, roughly 6 to 9 AM and 5 to 9 PM on weekdays. A caterer who has not planned around this arrives late not because they are careless, but because they scheduled against a map estimate instead of Metro Manila reality.

This is exactly why we vet Metro Manila caterers for early departure discipline: staff and equipment leaving well ahead of the buffer any map app suggests, specifically to absorb an EDSA slowdown without it touching your event start time. December is the worst month for this citywide, Christmas shopping and office parties add to already-heavy traffic on every major route.

What this means for your booking: if your venue sits across EDSA from where your caterer is based, ask directly how much lead time they build in, and whether their crew departs before or after the morning rush if your event starts early.

Common questions.

Before you ask

What areas of Metro Manila do you cover?

Our vetted bench covers the full metro, including Quezon City, Makati, Marikina, Paranaque, Pasig, Taguig, Mandaluyong, Caloocan and the surrounding cities. Tell us your venue location when you request a quote.

How much does catering cost in Metro Manila?

Rates vary by occasion and service style; wedding and milestone catering generally runs from around 700 pesos per head for a basic buffet up to 3,500 pesos and above per head for a premium plated service. We do not publish fixed prices, every quote is custom to your event.

How far in advance should I book catering in Metro Manila?

Given how much demand concentrates in the metro, especially during December and graduation season (March to April), booking as early as possible secures more choice from our vetted bench.

Can you cater a hotel ballroom event in Makati or BGC?

Yes, our bench includes caterers experienced with hotel and high-rise venue logistics specifically, loading docks, service elevators, kitchen access, which differ from a garden or open-air setup.
