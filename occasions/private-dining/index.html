The standard

Private event catering does not run on banquet logic. A wedding or a company party is judged on scale and flow; a private dinner is judged on courses, pacing and the feeling that the menu was written for this table, on this night, for these people. The room is smaller, so every dish is closer to every guest, and there is nowhere for a weak course to hide.

That changes what you need from a caterer. A private function catering brief needs a chef who can cook to restaurant standard in unfamiliar surroundings, service staff who read a table instead of managing a line, and a menu planned around your kitchen, your equipment and your guests, not around a reheating schedule.

Our private catering services are built for exactly that brief. We match you with vetted caterers and private chefs who have cooked in homes and private rooms before, and the same quote-and-guarantee that covers our biggest weddings covers your dinner for six. However you shape the night, private dinner catering is a course-led brief, and the bench is staffed for it.

By format
At home

Private chef, your table

Private catering in your own home is the format most people picture first: a private chef who shops, preps, cooks and plates in your kitchen, with your dining room as the restaurant. Private home catering lives or dies on the kitchen check, so the caterer confirms your range, oven, prep space and power during quoting instead of discovering problems on the night.

The menu is course-led rather than buffet-led, and the service staff count is kept to what the table actually needs, usually a chef plus one or two servers for a home dinner.

Catering watch-out: The best private chef catering services bring what is missing and say so in the quote, not on the day.
Dinner party

The private dinner party

A private dinner party catering brief covers the celebrations between everyday meals and big events: anniversaries, proposals, milestone birthdays, business dinners and reunion tables. Private party catering at this scale is judged on courses and pacing, so the menu reads like a restaurant tasting menu rather than a lineup of party trays.

Private catered dinner party formats can run plated, family-style or a hybrid, in your home or in a booked private room. Catering private dinner parties well means staffing to the room: intimate tables need servers who refold, refill and disappear, not a buffet crew.

Catering watch-out: Confirm the service style in the quote itself. Plated and family-style need different staff counts, and a quote that does not name the style is guessing.

Common questions.

Before you ask

What is private dining catering?

Private dining catering is a chef-led meal served in a private setting: your home, a private room or a booked venue, instead of a restaurant or a banquet hall. It ranges from a private catered dinner party for six to a private function with full service staff, and the menu is built around your table rather than around a buffet line.

Can a private chef cater in my own home?

Yes, that is the most requested private dining format. Private chef catering in your own home works in most residential kitchens; the caterer confirms your cooking range, oven space and prep area during quoting, then brings ingredients, any rented equipment and the service staff the menu needs. Very large menus may need one rented warming or cooking unit, which the quote lists.

How many guests does private dining suit?

Private dining suits the intimate end of the scale, roughly 2 to 50 guests. Below about 30, plated courses or family-style service beat a buffet, because a private dinner party catering setup is judged on courses and pacing rather than on variety. Larger private party catering requests can still work at home with the right staff count.

How much does private catering cost?

Private catering is quoted per event, not per menu card. The price moves with the menu's ingredients, the number of service staff, the courses, and whether equipment has to be brought in. We do not publish fixed prices; request a quote with your date, city and headcount and you receive one custom quote within 24 hours.
